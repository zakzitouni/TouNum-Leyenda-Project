From an unidentified image to a captioned photograph

Drop one or several digitised files. Each one goes through the complete workflow: binary classification sorts photographs from documents, diagrams, drawings and paintings; the photographs are cleaned by the denoiser; then a caption is written. The two versions below share the first two steps and differ only in the captioning model.
